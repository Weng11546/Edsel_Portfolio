Poultry Feed Stock Tracking with POS Application

Automate inventory management and sales transactions, improve the accuracy of stock records, and enhance the efficiency of operations in poultry feed businesses.

Project overview

Poultry Feed Stock Tracking and Inventory System integrated with a Point of Sale (POS) that will automate inventory management and sales transactions, improve the accuracy of stock records, and enhance the efficiency of operations in poultry feed businesses.

Problem statement

This study aims to develop a Poultry Feed Stock Tracking and Inventory System integrated with a Point of Sale (POS) to improve inventory management and sales operations. The study seeks to address the challenges associated with manual inventory tracking, inaccurate stock records, and inefficient sales monitoring.

Objectives

  • Develop a computerized inventory system to monitor poultry feed stock levels in real time.
  • Improve efficiency in sales and inventory management by minimizing manual record-keeping.
  • Enhance the accuracy of stock records and sales data.

Target users

  • Business Owners
  • Store Managers
  • Inventory Clerk
  • Cashiers

Technologies used

  • kotlin
  • Android SDK
  • Android Studio
  • Claude
  • Discord Webhooks & Bots

Key features

Discord Web Hooks and Bots

Create, read, update, and delete records via Discord api.

Search products

Help users find the product quickly.

Authentication

Login, roles, and session handling.

Validation

Input credentials and clear error messages.

System Design

The Poultry Feed Stock Tracking and Inventory System with POS is a computerized system designed to automate inventory management and sales transactions. It allows users to monitor stock levels, process sales, update inventory automatically, and generate reports efficiently
